# 5-minute Loom walkthrough script

Use this as a speaking guide rather than reading every word. Share your browser and show the desktop version first, then switch to a narrow/mobile view.

## 0:00–0:35 — Introduction

“Hi, Dr. Reynolds. Thank you for taking a look at this first draft of your website. I’ve designed it to feel calm, welcoming, and easy to navigate. The goal is to help a potential client understand the kind of support available and feel comfortable taking a first step.”

## 0:35–1:20 — Hero and navigation

“At the top, the navigation gives visitors a simple way to explore the approach, services, therapist introduction, office, and contact section. The main headline is intentionally reassuring, and the primary button gives visitors a clear next step. The large image and generous spacing help the page feel human rather than clinical.”

## 1:20–2:05 — Approach and values

“This section introduces the tone of the practice. Instead of overwhelming visitors with a lot of information at once, it highlights a few principles: being welcomed without judgment, moving at a comfortable pace, and finding support that fits the individual.”

## 2:05–2:45 — Services

“The three service cards make it easy to scan the areas where someone may be looking for support. Each card uses a short heading and a plain-language description. In the final content pass, these labels and descriptions should match your confirmed specialties and therapeutic approaches exactly.”

## 2:45–3:20 — About section

“The introduction is designed to feel personal and approachable. The copy focuses on collaboration and a supportive relationship, while the image adds warmth and visual balance. The biography and any credentials should be updated from your supplied profile so every detail is accurate.”

## 3:20–4:00 — Our Office

“I added a dedicated Our Office section, which is a new part of the page. It gives visitors a sense of the environment before they arrive and reinforces comfort, privacy, and welcome. The final version should use the actual office details and any images provided in your profile.”

## 4:00–4:30 — FAQs and contact

“The FAQ section answers common first-step questions in a compact, expandable format, so the page stays easy to scan. The closing call to action reminds visitors that they don’t need to have everything figured out before reaching out.”

## 4:30–5:00 — Mobile view and close

“Here is the mobile layout. The sections stack vertically, the navigation becomes a compact menu, and the buttons remain easy to tap. I’ve kept the same colour palette and visual style across screen sizes. Thank you for reviewing this first draft. I’d be happy to refine the content and details based on your feedback.”

## Recording tips

- Speak slowly and naturally; do not rush to finish exactly on each timestamp.
- Actually click the mobile menu and open/close an FAQ during the demo.
- Show the page at a desktop width and a mobile width.
- Only describe profile-specific facts after verifying them against the complete Dr. Reynolds profile.
